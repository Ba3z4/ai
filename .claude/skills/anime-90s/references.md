# Fuentes de la skill anime-90s

Consultadas por búsqueda web; los sitios completos no se pudieron abrir, así que lo citado son los resúmenes de los resultados.

## Arte, película y animación
- [The 90s anime aesthetics: exploring its timeless appeal (AnimeHunch)](https://animehunch.com/the-90s-anime-aesthetics-exploring-its-timeless-appeal/): cel pintado sobre fondos pintados, grano, calidez, líneas gruesas.
- [Ask John: Why Does Pre-Digital Era Anime Have an Aged Look? (AnimeNation)](https://www.animenation.net/blog/?p=26264): por qué el anime en cel se ve envejecido y con ruido.
- [Ask John: Is Today's Digital Anime Inferior to Vintage Analog Anime? (AnimeNation)](https://www.animenation.net/blog/?p=1086)
- [Stepping inside a retro anime inspired game (Unreal Engine tech blog)](https://www.unrealengine.com/tech-blog/stepping-inside-a-retro-anime-inspired-game-a-look-into-the-rendering-of-orbitals): sombreado en pocos valores, halación, grano.
- [Limited Animation: What is it? (GarageFarm)](https://garagefarm.net/blog/limited-animation-what-is-it) y [What Is Limited Animation? (Pixune)](https://pixune.com/blog/what-is-limited-animation/): cuadros sostenidos, partes animadas sobre cel fijo, panorámicas.
- [Shot on threes (Anime News Network)](https://AnimeNewsNetwork.com/encyclopedia/lexicon.php?id=61): animar en treses (8 dibujos por segundo).
- [Stillness and Style in Neon Genesis Evangelion (William Routt)](https://evamonkey.com/writings/routt-william-stillness-and-style-in-neon-genesis-evangelion.php) y [EvaGeeks](https://wiki.evageeks.org/Neon_Genesis_Evangelion): planos fijos larguísimos como recurso.
- [What Kazuo Oga Thinks About When He Thinks About Backgrounds (Animation Obsessive)](https://animationobsessive.substack.com/p/what-kazuo-oga-thinks-about-when) y [The painting process of Kazuo Oga (Open Culture)](https://www.openculture.com/2021/01/a-look-inside-the-painting-process-of-the-studio-ghibli-artist-kazuo-oga.html): gouache de poster color, capa base en una sola sesión.
- [The Art of Ghost in the Shell / Hiromasa Ogura (Parka Blogs)](https://parkablogs.com/node/2985): fondos de acuarela inspirados en fotos de Hong Kong, con halos de farolas.
- [Perfect Blue cinematography analysis (Color Culture)](https://colorculture.org/perfect-blue-cinematography-analysis/): realismo sobrio, azules saturados con luz artificial.
- [Serial Experiments Lain (Anime News Network review)](https://animenewsnetwork.com/review/lain-dvd): sombras negras profundas, días casi blancos, noches negras con luces de neón.
- [Diseño de personajes 1990s vs 2000s (Stuttgart Media)](https://jvmg.iuk.hdm-stuttgart.de/2023/06/05/a-tiny-use-case-approach-to-a-preliminary-overview-of-formal-and-stylistic-transformations-of-character-designs-between-the-1990s-and-2000s/) y [Animerica Interview: Yoshiyuki Sadamoto](https://www.evamonkey.com/writings/animerica-interview-yoshiyuki-sadamoto.php): diseño más sobrio y refinado; figuras delgadas y pálidas.

## Sonido
- [Why Is the Music in Cowboy Bebop so Damn Good? (Fandom)](https://www.fandom.com/articles/why-is-the-music-in-cowboy-bebop-so-damn-good): jazz-fusion; se cita solo como contraste (no es el tono de esta pieza).
- [Beyond Akira: Bwana's guide to the best anime OSTs (Electronic Beats)](https://www.electronicbeats.net/beyond-akira-bwanas-guide-best-anime-osts)
- [Kenji Kawai: Ghost in the Shell OST](https://www.sorrystaterecords.com/products/201021954): coro cantando en armonías de folk búlgaro sin vibrato, sobre percusión antigua y sintetizador.
- [Shirō Sagisu (AnimeClick)](https://www.animeclick.it/autore/319/shirō-sagisu): banda sonora de Evangelion, con cantos y violines chillones en *The End of Evangelion*.
